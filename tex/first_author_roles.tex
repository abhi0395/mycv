% 6: Anand et al. 2026 (NaI Paper)

Led the first large blind survey of intervening Na I absorbers and measured their low-redshift incidence and cosmic evolution.

% 5: Anand et al. 2025 (CIV Paper)

Constructed the largest C IV absorber catalogue to date and measured the evolution of its incidence and cosmic mass density over $1.4<z<4.5$.

% 4: Anand et al. 2024 (Archetype Paper)

Developed and implemented a new DESI galaxy redshift-fitting method that reduced catastrophic failures and improved redshift reliability.

% 3: Anand et al. 2022 (Cool Gas in Clusters)

Mapped cool metal-enriched gas around galaxy clusters and showed that clusters host a substantial reservoir of cool gas in the intracluster medium.

% 2: Anand et al. 2021 (Cool Gas in SDSS Galaxies)

Constructed a large SDSS Mg II absorber sample to characterize the spatial distribution and kinematics of cool circumgalactic gas around galaxies.

% 1: Anand et al. 2019 (Superdisks Paper)

Analysed VLA observations and led the interpretation and writing of the H I 21 cm study conducted as part of my MS thesis.
